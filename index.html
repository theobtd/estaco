About Us

Estaco is more than just a platform—it’s a return to what truly matters.

Born in the heart of Provence, France, Estaco (which means “relationship” in Provencal) was created by our founder as a way to stay connected with family and friends, no matter where life takes us. After moving abroad for studies and work, the need for a space that fosters genuine, meaningful connections became clear.

A Space for What Matters
On Estaco, you’re limited to just 15 friends. This isn’t a restriction—it’s a celebration of focus. Here, you’ll only ever see what your friends share, and your posts will only ever be seen by them. There are no influencers, no noise, and no endless scroll. Just the people who matter most.

Privacy by Design
Your privacy is at the core of Estaco. We believe in a world where you control who sees your life, and you only see what truly matters to you. No algorithms, no ads, no distractions—just you and your circle.

Get in Touch
Estaco is a labor of love, and we’re always here to listen. If you have questions, feedback, or just want to say hello, reach out directly to our founder at tdgb@estaco.org. You won’t find a generic inbox—just a real person, ready to connect.
